About Uganda World Connect

Uganda World Connect is a general information platform connecting readers with stories, knowledge and useful information from Uganda, Africa and around the world.

The website can grow over time with articles, original photos, community stories, educational resources, business information, technology, IT and virtual assistance resources, and other useful content.
